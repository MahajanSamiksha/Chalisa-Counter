Privacy Policy for Chalisa Counter

Effective date: 2026-10-05

Chalisa Counter is provided by Samiksha Mahajan. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information stored by the app

Chalisa Counter stores your daily recitation counts on your device to display your progress across the 40-day cycle. These counts are saved in private app storage.

The app does not require an account. It does not use advertising, analytics or tracking services, and does not send your counts to the developer.

Android backup and device transfer

Depending on your device and Android backup settings, Android may back up your counts to cloud storage or transfer them to another device. Restoring a backup may restore previously saved counts.

These features are managed by Android and your backup provider. Their terms and privacy policies apply. You can manage backup settings through your device settings.

Optional exports

When you tap Export and choose a destination, the app saves a text report containing your daily counts, total progress and export date.

You choose where the report is saved. If you select a cloud storage provider, that provider may upload and store the report under its own terms and privacy policy. The developer does not receive a copy through the app.

Exported reports contain information about your devotional practice. Consider this before saving them to shared storage or sharing them with others.

Storage and security

Counts are stored in the app’s private storage using Android’s application storage protections. For exports, the app uses the document access granted through Android’s system file picker.

Exported files are stored outside the app’s private storage. Their access controls and security depend on the destination you choose. Android backups are protected according to your device settings and backup provider’s practices.

Retention and deletion

Counts remain in app storage until you reset them, clear the app’s storage through Android settings or uninstall the app.

Resetting counts does not delete exported reports or existing backups. You must delete exported reports from their saved locations and manage backups through your device settings or backup provider. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove these external copies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s functionality or data-handling practices change. The updated policy will show a revised effective date.
